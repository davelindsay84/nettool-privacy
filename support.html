Common questions

Why does Net Tool ask for my location?

iOS only tells apps the name of your Wi-Fi network if they have location access. Net Tool uses it for that alone and never stores where you are. Everything else works without it.

Why doesn't it show my Wi-Fi signal strength?

iOS doesn't let apps read Wi-Fi signal strength. Net Tool measures what matters instead: how quickly and reliably your router answers.

The device scan or Cameras page missed something.

Some devices don't answer on the network at all, and cloud cameras (such as Ring or Nest) often talk only to their own servers. Make sure Local Network access is on in Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network.
