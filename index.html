Stringless Net Tool — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-25. Published by White Rice Games LLC. Contact: dave@exigence.net · Support

Stringless Net Tool ("Net Tool") checks your internet connection and your home network. We built it to work without knowing who you are: there is no account, no sign-up, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. This policy explains, in plain language, what the app does with data.

The short version

  • We collect nothing. White Rice Games has no server for this app and receives no data from it: no usage statistics, no crash reports, no identifiers.
  • Everything the app measures or remembers (history, saved hosts, home maps, findings) is stored on your device only.
  • To test your connection, the app has to talk to some well-known internet services (for example a speed-test server). Those services see your public IP address, as with any website visit. The app lists every one of them, with what it sends and when, under Help › What Left Your iPhone.
  • We never sell data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

Data stored on your device only

Results history, the connection diary and "Network Over Time" findings, saved hosts and lists, Find Bad Spots maps and room scans, settings, and a count of tips and words of encouragement you've sent. You can delete it all by deleting the app. If you use iCloud device backups, it is included in your backup like any app's data; we cannot read it.

Internet services the app contacts

Only when a feature needs it, and only to measure your connection. Depending on what you use and the providers you choose in Settings, these include:

  • Connection checks: a ping and connectivity service (Cloudflare by default), Apple's hotspot sign-in check, and a lookup of your public IP address.
  • DNS: your network's own DNS resolver, plus Cloudflare, Google and Akamai for comparison and to spot leaks.
  • Speed tests and load tests (including Find Bad Spots' load modes): the speed-test server you choose (Cloudflare by default).
  • Network names: Team Cymru or RIPEstat, to name the company behind an address; registries (IANA, ARIN, RIPE NCC and others) for Whois.
  • Worldwide site check: Globalping probes, only when you check whether a site is down for everyone.
  • What Works Here, calls and gaming checks: port, STUN and HTTP/3 test servers, game regions (Valve, Microsoft Azure, Amazon Web Services), and your mobile carrier's Wi-Fi Calling gateway (a connection handshake only; nothing is logged in).
  • Hosts you type: tools such as Ping, Traceroute and Ports contact only the host you enter.

These services receive your public IP address and the test traffic itself. They are run by their own operators under their own privacy policies; Net Tool sends them nothing that identifies you.

Your local network

Device scans, the Cameras page and router checks talk only to devices on your own network, and only when you open those screens. Nothing found on your network leaves your device. The app never tries to log in to any device.

Permissions

  • Local Network: to find devices and test your router.
  • Location (optional): iOS only reveals the Wi-Fi network's name to apps with location access. Net Tool uses it for that alone and never stores or shares where you are.
  • Camera (optional): Find Bad Spots uses the camera and LiDAR to place Wi-Fi readings on a map of your home, or to photograph a floor plan. Nothing is recorded or uploaded; scans stay on your device.
  • Bluetooth (optional): to list Bluetooth devices nearby. It connects to a device only when you ask.
  • Notifications (optional): only if you turn on background checks.

Tips

The optional Tip Jar uses Apple's in-app purchase system. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details or Apple ID. Tips unlock nothing.

Children

Net Tool is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
